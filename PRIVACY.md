# GroceMaxxer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 September 2026_

GroceMaxxer is a grocery checklist app. It is free, contains no advertising,
and makes no money. This policy explains what it does with your information.
The short version: **it doesn't collect any.**

## What we collect

Nothing. GroceMaxxer has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no
crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK. It requests no Android
permissions at all.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into GroceMaxxer stays on your device:

- your grocery lists, the items on them and which ones you have checked off,
  kept per calendar date;
- your settings — selected store, accent colour and dark-mode preference;
- a built-in catalogue of common grocery items that ships with the app.

This is written to the app's own private storage area, which no other app on
your device can read. It is never transmitted anywhere, because the app has no
internet permission and makes no network connections.

## Sharing a list

The "Share" button hands the text of your list to Android's system share
sheet, and you choose what happens next — a message, an email, the clipboard,
or nothing. GroceMaxxer does not send that text anywhere itself and keeps no
copy outside your device. Whatever app you pick is then governed by that app's
own privacy policy.

Importing works the same way in reverse: you paste text in, and it is parsed
on your device.

## Android backup

If you have Android's backup enabled in your system settings, your lists and
settings may be included in your device backup, the same way as for other
apps. That backup is between you and Google (or your device manufacturer)
under their privacy policy; the developer of GroceMaxxer has no access to it.
You can turn backup off in your device's system settings.

## Children

GroceMaxxer is not directed at children, and because it collects no data it
collects no data from children either.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling GroceMaxxer, or using "Clear storage" on its entry in Android's
app settings, permanently removes everything the app has stored. There is
nothing held anywhere else to delete.
