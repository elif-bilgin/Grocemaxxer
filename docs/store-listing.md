# Play Store listing copy

Paste-ready text for the Play Console listing. Character limits are Play's.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
GroceMaxxer
```

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Your grocery run, optimized — items sorted into one walk through your store.
```

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
GroceMaxxer turns your shopping list into a route.

Type in what you need and GroceMaxxer groups the items by the part of the
store they live in, then orders those groups into one sensible walk — produce
near the entrance, frozen just before the checkout, and so on. Check things
off in the order they appear and you shouldn't have to double back.

PICK YOUR STORE
Every store is laid out differently, so the walking order changes with it.
Choose Safeway, Whole Foods, Target or Trader Joe's and the sections reorder
to match that chain's real layout, including the cart-packing rules of thumb:
heavy dry goods first, produce on top of them, delicate bakery items last.
No store selected? You get a sensible generic route.

BUILT TO BE FAST TO USE
- Start typing and suggestions appear from a built-in catalogue of around 250
  common grocery items, already filed under the right section.
- Not sure what to call it? Browse by aisle and tap to add.
- Duplicates are skipped automatically and names are tidied up as you type.
- Got the category wrong? Override it when you add the item.

CHECKING OFF FEELS GOOD
Checked items sink to the bottom of their section, and a section you've
finished folds away into a tidy stack at the bottom of the screen. Clear the
last item and the whole list celebrates with you.

YOUR LISTS, BY DAY
Lists are kept per date. Start a fresh one, pick up where you left off, or
pull up the list from any earlier shopping day — it gets copied to today, so
your history stays intact.

SHARE A LIST WITH WHOEVER IS SHOPPING
Send your list as a plain, readable message through any app you already use.
When someone sends you one, GroceMaxxer spots it on your clipboard, shows you
exactly how it differs from your list, and lets you overwrite, merge item by
item, or ignore it.

MAKE IT YOURS
Four pastel accent colours and a dark mode.

NO ACCOUNT, NO INTERNET, NO ADS
GroceMaxxer has no sign-in, asks for no permissions, and has no internet
access at all. Everything lives on your phone. It's free, it has no adverts,
and it collects nothing about you — there is nothing to collect it with.
```

## Notes

- The em dash in the short description is intentional; Play accepts it.
- The full description mentions the store names descriptively. It makes no
  claim of affiliation, which keeps it inside Play's impersonation and
  intellectual-property policies. Do not add store logos to the listing
  graphics.
- "around 250" tracks `PresetCatalog.items`; update it if the catalogue grows
  substantially.
